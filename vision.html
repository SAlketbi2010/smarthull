SMARTHULL AND THE UAE

From UAE maritime goals
to a strain sensor.

The UAE wants to be a global maritime power with a safer, smarter and more digital sector. This page sets out the maritime goals, laws and standards we found, the technical need each one implies, and what SmartHull has and has not shown against it.

Scope

SmartHull is a classroom-scale prototype: a plastic optical fiber on a polycarbonate plate, read by an ESP32. The connections below describe direction, not demonstrated national impact. Plans and targets are quoted from the sources listed at the bottom of the page.

HOW SMARTHULL CONNECTS TO THE UAE, IN ONE LINE

Low-cost structural-strain sensing for composite hulls, aimed at the UAE’s smart, safe and digital maritime sector.

Our research question is: can an affordable optical sensor give repeatable estimates of bending strain? A hull that reports its own strain is the use we are aiming at; today we have shown it only on a 2 mm polycarbonate plate in a laboratory.

UAE MARITIME GOALS

What the UAE has set out
for its maritime sector.

These are the maritime goals, plans and rules we found, with the source and what each one says. Where a source does not state a safety or structural-monitoring target, we say so.

  1. A “global maritime power”, with intelligent transport.

    The Minister of Energy and Infrastructure, Suhail bin Mohammed Al Mazrouei, has described the aim of a more sustainable and intelligent maritime transport system, supported by digitalisation, decarbonisation in line with IMO objectives, and drafting regulatory best practice for autonomous vessels. The same account gives no specific safety targets.

  2. Abu Dhabi Maritime Plan 2031.

    Work on the plan begins in 2027, with a target of a top-20 place in the Leading Maritime Cities of the World ranking (22nd in 2024). It is built on 10 value pools, including shipbuilding, with 18 objectives, 49 measurable KPIs and 96 initiatives.

  3. Federal Decree-Law No. 43 of 2023 (Maritime Law).

    In force from 29 March 2024. The Ship Register records a vessel’s type, size and classification; the Ministry of Energy and Infrastructure approves specifications and drawings for new builds; and there is a register of ships under construction. Executive regulations were still pending in mid-2024.

  4. IMO interim guidelines for FRP in ship structures.

    MSC.1/Circ.1574 (2017) covers fire safety of fibre-reinforced plastic elements. The guidance itself notes it does not fully address progressive structural collapse. It sets no strain limits, and neither the UAE nor the IMO sources we found set one for hull monitoring.

Industry context: UAE yards already build large composite vessels. Gulf Craft, in Umm Al Quwain, unveiled the 54 m Majesty 175, described as the largest composite yacht in production, built from carbon fibre and vinyl ester.

UAE CENTENNIAL 2071

The best country in the world
by 2071.

The Centennial 2071 plan was launched by Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum as a roadmap for the five decades after 2021. Its goals include preparing future generations for rapid change and reducing dependence on oil by diversifying the economy. It has four pillars:

  1. A future-focused government.

    A global model with forward-looking leadership.

  2. Excellent education.

    A priority on advanced science, technology, engineering and innovation, with schools as “incubators of entrepreneurship and innovation”.

  3. A diversified knowledge economy.

    Higher productivity, investment in research and more homegrown inventors and scientists.

  4. A happy and cohesive society.

    A secure, tolerant and cohesive society with a high quality of life.

WE THE UAE 2031

A ten-year plan for
society, economy and safety.

Announced on 22 November 2022 at the UAE Annual Government Meetings, We the UAE 2031 sets four pillars and economic targets for the decade to 2031:

  1. Forward Society.

    Top 10 in the Human Development Index, with its cities among the world’s 10 best.

  2. Forward Economy.

    GDP of AED 3 trillion, non-oil exports of AED 800 billion and foreign trade of AED 4 trillion.

  3. Forward Diplomacy.

    Stronger international relations and a role in the global environmental agenda, including climate neutrality.

  4. Forward Ecosystem.

    First in the world on the safety index, top three in cybersecurity and top 10 in food security, with proactive legislation for new economic sectors.

OPERATION 300BN

A bigger, more advanced
industrial base.

The UAE industrial strategy aims to raise the industrial sector’s contribution to GDP from AED 133 billion to AED 300 billion by 2031. Its initiatives include catalysing the adoption of fourth-industrial-revolution technology to raise the productivity of anchor industries, with 11 priority sectors including rubber and plastics, machinery and equipment, and medical and space technology.

NEXT STEPS

What would make the link stronger.

Strain measured with a gauge instead of calculated, tests on a real composite coupon, more stops to map the curve, temperature and long-term drift tests, and an on-board ESP32 estimator. These are the same next steps described in the Phase 2 results on the project page.
