Equations and interpretation

Sensing fiber routing
One continuous sensing path crosses the plate, turns around and returns across it. The two passes are not independent sensing channels; the reference loop is separate. Pass spacing, turn radius and attachment details were not measured. Routing dimensions are illustrative; no factor-of-two strain or loss correction is applied.

Reference normalization
R = VD35/VD34; q (%) = 100(1 − R/R₀). Medians of sample-wise ratios were recorded; no 142 mV floor subtraction was applied. Historical single-channel q = 100(1 − V/V₀).

Fitting error
RMSE = √[Σ(qᵢ − q̂ᵢ)²/n], in percentage points. Five trial summaries are not five sets of independent sample-level replicates. Each reference-channel acquisition lasted about eight seconds, summarizing approximately 92 distinct rows including the initial rolling buffer.

Finite-rotation mechanics
κ = |w″|/(1 + w′²)3/2; |εᵦ| = (t/2)κ. For t = 2 mm and L = 228.6 mm, the smooth-support central-point-load elastica gives 2,278, 4,447 and 6,414 µε at 10, 20 and 30 mm. The model assumes an initially straight, uniform, inextensible beam able to slide over frictionless supports. Broad hand contact, support friction and plate effects are excluded. Strain values are conditional centre bending estimates, not measured fiber strain.

All-trial voltage-to-calculated-strain mapping
q ≈ 0.0005566 εcalc − 0.1209; εcalc (µε) ≈ (q + 0.1209)/0.0005566. This is a separate all-five-trial fit to model labels; the filtered deflection fit does not establish a filtered strain calibration.

For support slope φ: Iₓ = ∫₀^φ cosθ/√sin(φ−θ) dθ and Iᵧ = ∫₀^φ sinθ/√sin(φ−θ) dθ, with upper limit φ. Solve Iᵧ/Iₓ = 2δ/L, then εb,c = (t/L)Iₓ√sinφ. The report details numerical checks.

Overview and Phase 2

Phase 1 recorded optical-path sensitivity, reversible direct-fiber responses and a preliminary association between reported plate deflection and normalized receiver response. The 10 mm and repeated 20 mm cycles recovered close to their baselines. The complete data and supplementary filtered analysis are retained for engineering review.

Next: secure the mechanical arrangement, repeat fixed-deflection cycles, independently verify strain labels and assess reference correction under controlled source changes. Compare linear and nonlinear estimators using whole held-out cycles, then deploy a validated lightweight model on the ESP32. TinyML and marine hull monitoring remain future work.
